Privacy Policy for Marlin

Last updated: June 2026

Marlin ("the app") is a marine species identification and life-list app developed by Fiona Wille. This policy explains what data the app accesses, how it's used, and what is — and isn't — collected or shared.

Summary

Marlin does not require an account, does not use analytics, advertising, or tracking of any kind, and does not collect or transmit your personal data to the developer. Everything you log — sightings, notes, photos, and locations — stays on your device, under your control.

Location

Marlin requests access to your device's location (ACCESS_FINE_LOCATION / ACCESS_COARSE_LOCATION) to show you marine species commonly recorded near you, and to optionally tag the location of a sighting you log.

  • Your coordinates are sent only to the iNaturalist API to retrieve nearby-species data — they are not sent to, or stored by, the developer.
  • You can decline location access entirely and use the manual location override in Settings ("as if I'm at…") instead.
  • Location data attached to a sighting is stored only in the app's local database on your device.

Photos

You may attach photos to a sighting from your device's photo library. The permission Marlin requests is used solely to let you pick and attach images — Marlin does not access your photo library for any other purpose.

Photos you attach are resized and copied into the app's private storage on your device. They are never uploaded to the developer or any third party.

Your life list data

Sightings, notes, photos, and locations you log are stored in a local database on your device. This data:

  • Is never transmitted to the developer or to any third party
  • May be included in Android Auto Backup, a standard Android OS feature that backs up app data to your own Google account (you can disable this in Settings)
  • Can be manually exported to, or imported from, a file of your choosing, entirely under your control

Third-party services

Marlin makes read-only requests to the following external services to show you species information. No personal data is sent to them beyond what's described above.

ServicePurposeData sent
iNaturalist API Species data, photos, observations, nearby-species lookup Approximate coordinates (only for "nearby species" lookups), species search terms
Wikipedia API Species summary text shown on species detail pages Species name
IUCN Red List API Conservation status badge (optional feature) Species name

These are independent services with their own privacy policies; Marlin has no control over how they handle requests it sends them.

What Marlin does not do

  • No account or sign-up is required, and Marlin does not collect your name, email, or any other personal identifier
  • No analytics, advertising, or crash-reporting SDKs are included in the app
  • No data is sold, rented, or shared with advertisers or data brokers
  • No data is collected from or about children

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted at this same URL with a revised "Last updated" date.
